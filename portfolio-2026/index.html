KEEP BUILDING / KEEP LEARNING

I'm curious about what's possible.
Then I build it.

I’m Ayomide David Oladeji, but you can call me Xpen. I'm a computer science graduate from Olabisi Onabanjo University and a software engineer who enjoys working from the first messy idea all the way to real interfaces, APIs, and systems.

I founded Hustle to explore how skilled people can turn what they know into economic opportunity. That same curiosity keeps pulling me into human-controlled AI, financial tools, spatial intelligence, and better ways of building software.

I'm comfortable moving between product thinking, design, frontend, backend, databases, integrations, and experimentation. I believe a good engineer should understand not just how a thing works, but why it matters.
