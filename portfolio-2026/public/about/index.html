A product-minded engineer who enjoys going from a messy idea to a usable system. I work across frontend, backend, design, AI workflows and building products.

Based inLagos, Nigeria
EducationB.Sc. Computer Science · 2025
FocusProducts · Engineering · AI

I founded Hustle to explore how people who have real-world skills can be discovered and turn their expertise into opportunity. That question led me into marketplaces, better booking experiences and the systems that make transactions trustworthy.

The same curiosity has taken me into human-controlled AI agents, fintech tools and experiments that connect software with the physical world. My interest isn't just how an interface looks; it's what happens when people depend on the system behind it.

I share unfinished thoughts, lessons and experiments here because I want the journey to be visible—not only its polished outcomes.
